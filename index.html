About Revathy

More Than a Volunteer — A Community Builder.

Revathy Venkat volunteers across two of Singapore's major programmes — Families for Life (FFL) as a Family Ambassador, and Team Nila under Sport Singapore. Together, she has clocked 208 confirmed service hours across 88 sessions and 67 unique events in 2025–2026.

With FFL, she engages parents and children at community events across Punggol, Sengkang, Tampines, Pasir Ris, and beyond — helping families connect with resources that strengthen them. With Team Nila, she has served at world-class events including the World Aquatics Championships, Standard Chartered Marathon (overnight!), SBH International Challenge, and the upcoming NBA Rising Stars Invitational 2026.

She also volunteered at two SG60 National Day events in August 2025 — a proud reflection of her commitment to the community that shaped her.

The Journey

2025–2026
Service Record

May–Jun 2025
National Family Festival 2025 @ Expo

Volunteered across two shifts at NFF 2025 (29 May & 1 Jun) — Singapore's flagship family celebration at Expo. Part of the Family Ambassadors team engaging thousands of families over the weekend.

Jun–Aug 2025
World Aquatics Championships & Community Events

Served as Team Nila Front-of-House volunteer at the World Aquatics Masters Championships (11 & 17 Aug), cheered at Water Polo matches, and clocked hours at PA PLAYGround Punggol and Tampines OTH Festival through the summer.

Aug 2025
SG60 National Day — Two Events

Punggol Shore National Day Fiesta (6 Aug, evening till night, 3.5h) and Fernvale SG60 National Day Celebrations (8 Aug, 4h). Two nights, two communities, one proud diamond jubilee for Singapore.

Sep–Nov 2025
Big Autumn Run of Events

Deepavali goodie pack packing (Farrer Park, 2 days), Big Heart 10-niversary at Pasir Ris, Mendaki Family Play Date, World Amateur Team Championships (Team Nila, 3 confirmed days, 19.5h), REAL Run, Stradale Kindness Showcase, and the Standard Chartered Marathon overnight shifts — 6 & 7 December, 1am–8am.

Sports Volunteering & Community Milestones

Kicked off 2026 with the SBH International Challenge (8h), Wings Cross Country (5h), Mega Triple P at Sengkang (4.5h), and the Singapore Minime event (5.5h). Also volunteered at the Pasir Ris EMB Ceremony and McDonald's Lohei Event in Punggol.

Apr–Jun 2026

Confirmed for three shifts at NFF 2026 @ Expo (29–31 May), ReadySetLearn Roadshow (25 Apr), FIBA 3x3 Asia Cup, NBA Rising Stars Invitational, NTUC First Campus Learning Festival, and more. The calendar keeps filling — the commitment never wavers.
